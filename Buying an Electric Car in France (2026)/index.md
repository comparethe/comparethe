# Buying an Electric Car in France (2026)

I'm in the market for an electric car in France — new or secondhand, open budget, no segment preference. Rather than wade through spammy SEO listicles and manufacturer marketing, I'm trying to do this the CompareThe＿ way: facts and figures, sourced, with a proper price-vs-quality efficient frontier instead of "it depends on your needs."

This is a working research log, not a finished comparison — think of it as the diary version, which will get turned into the real writeup below once the analysis is further along.

## The research so far

- [**Tax incentives**](01-tax-incentives.md) — bonus écologique, the EU-battery surbonus, leasing social, and why the malus doesn't apply to EVs.
- [**Model & TCO survey**](02-model-tco-survey.md) — city car to premium SUV, new prices, running costs, and why depreciation (not sticker price) dominates total cost of ownership. Includes a data-quality correction worth reading if you're pulling specs from "from €X, up to Ykm" marketing copy elsewhere.
- [**Price vs. range chart**](03-price-range-sketch.html) — an interactive scatter of price against WLTP range for 22 EV trims sold in France ([data](data-ev-models.json)). Range stands in for "quality" here only because it's the one spec reported consistently — not a real weighted score yet.
