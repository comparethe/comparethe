Electric Car Research & Purchase — working sketch

Starting price vs. range, 22 EVs sold in France

Range (WLTP) stands in for "quality" here because it's the one spec that's consistently reported across the market — not a weighted score. Price is the new sticker price, not a full TCO figure: depreciation, incentives and running costs aren't priced in yet.

Hollow points are figures I couldn't confirm directly this session (typical/estimated) — filled points are sourced. Treat this as a first cut for shortlisting, not a final number.

Correction (2026-08-09): the Kia EV3 and BYD Dolphin Surf were originally each plotted as one point pairing a base trim's starting price with a higher trim's max range ("from €X, up to Ykm" language glued two different cars together). Both are now split into their real trims — Kia EV3 Air (€35,990/436km) vs. Earth Long Range (€44,990/571km); BYD Dolphin Surf Active (€19,990/220km) vs. Boost (€24,490/322km). Worth the same scrutiny on any "from/up to" spec you read elsewhere.
On reading the trend forward: a chunk of today's steep EV depreciation is Tesla cutting its own new prices, not the market souring — and that pressure looks structural (Chinese manufacturing overcapacity), not a one-off that reverses. Rising battery raw-material costs pull the other way. Net: don't treat current used discounts as a floor, but they're not obviously a blip either.
